Why moringa?

Malunggay is a common plant in the Philippines and is widely eaten for its vitamins and minerals.

Real leaves

The snack is made from malunggay leaves, not just flavoring.

Light and crunchy

A crisp bite that works as a snack between classes or work.

Local ingredient

Malunggay grows easily here, so the main ingredient is familiar and easy to source.

What's inside

Ingredients: moringa leaves, rice flour, tapioca starch, vegetable oil, salt, and natural seasonings.

Net weight: 50 g, about 3 to 4 servings.

Storage: keep in a cool, dry place and seal tightly after opening.

The pack label also lists nutrition facts per 15 g serving.

How it's made

From leaf to pack, in four simple steps.

  1. Pick and wash fresh malunggay leaves.
  2. Season and prepare the leaves.
  3. Cook until crispy.
  4. Cool, pack, and seal.
